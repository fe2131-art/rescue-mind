\subsection{Study QI: Generated Images Scored by the Frozen Gate}\label{sec:moved-qi}
The paper's weakest points are the simulator-to-reality gap and the small hard-class sample. Study QI (registered before any generated study image or score; Amendments 1--4 recorded before any score) uses an image-editing model, Qwen-Image-2.1-Turbo (7\,B, 8 steps, fp16, one checkpoint, one seed per image), to make the depot test images of XD look photographic and to draw fires the simulator did not render, and scores them with the \emph{frozen} gate (Qwen3.5-9B, v3.6, $\tau=0.088$ from XD's own calibration; nothing is tuned). Related work uses generative editing to enlarge robot training data~\cite{yu2023rosie}, to translate simulator frames toward real ones~\cite{alhaija2025cosmostransfer,samak2025sim2realdiffusion} and to make simulated scenes more realistic for policy evaluation~\cite{benarous2025spie,li2024simpler}; synthetic smoke datasets show a clear synthetic-to-real gap~\cite{gaba2025wildfiresynthetic}. We know of no earlier use of a generative editor to stress-test a VLM safety gate under a registered, pixel-checked protocol; the claim is that narrow.
\emph{Sets.} QI-R: all XD test frames (views F, L, R) edited by one materials prompt (\QIRN{} perceivable hard-class fire worlds, \QIRCleanN{} clean worlds). QI-G: the clean twin of each XD fire world with a fire drawn only inside the fire world's own dilated fire mask (pixels outside the mask unchanged, checked; an image with less than 2\% flame colour in the mask is excluded and listed). QI-C: the same twins and masks with a neutral prompt (the editor's artifacts alone). A world is scored by the maximum over its three views; a view that was not drawn keeps the clean twin's own score (Amendment 3). Descriptive extensions (Amendment 2): a larger fire (G2) and two orange non-fire look-alikes (K1 vest, K2 warning lamp).
\emph{Claims.} QI1: the miss on the realised hard fires is at most 20 of 126 (XD's own: \QIBaseMiss{} of \QIBaseN): \QIRMiss{} of \QIRN{} (exact upper bound \QIRUpper), \QIClaimQIOne. QI2: the generated fire is missed in at most 20\% of worlds, unless more than 15\% of the generated fire images lack any flame colour in the mask (then the claim stops): \QIGExcl{} of \QIGGen{} images (\QIGExclShare) were excluded, so QI2 is \QIGStopped{} and \QIClaimQITwo; on the \QIGN{} remaining worlds \QIGMiss{} are missed (\QIGRate, upper \QIGUpper; descriptive, a selected subset). QI3: at most 5 false stops among the realised clean worlds, \QIRFalse{} of \QIRCleanN{}, \QIClaimQIThree; control edits cross $\tau$ in \QICFlips{} of \QICN{} twins (at most 5\%), \QIClaimQIThreec.
\emph{Extensions (descriptive, Amendment 2; no claim).} A larger generated fire (G2) is missed in \QIGTwoK{} of \QIGTwoN{} scored worlds (upper \QIGTwoUpper), so the gate does see large photographic fires once the editor draws them (the other twins are excluded by the same pixel check as QI-G). The two orange look-alikes cross $\tau$ in \QIKOneK{} of \QIKOneN{} worlds (a vest) and \QIKTwoK{} of \QIKTwoN{} (a glowing warning lamp), against \QICFlips{} of \QICN{} (\QICRate) for the neutral control: the vest adds nothing to the control's own artifact, the lamp raises the flag rate from \QICRate{} to \QIKTwoRate, consistent with a bright orange light source being read as fire.
\emph{Reading.} Three of the four claims fail (QI2 is stopped by its own rule); QI3 holds. \textbf{Photographic rendering costs the gate recall.} After realisation the gate misses \QIRMiss{} of \QIRN{} hard-class worlds against \QIBaseMiss{} on the simulator frames (QI1 \QIClaimQIOne); \QIRBoth{} worlds are missed in both, \QIRRecovered{} are recovered and \QIRNew{} are newly missed. On the worlds whose fire cue the pixel audit finds kept the rate is \QIRKeptMiss{} of \QIRKeptN{} (\QIRKeptRate), so most of the rise is not the editor deleting the fire. Looking at the 18 new misses by eye (scores known), in about twelve the simulator's grey translucent smoke disc is still there, now on a photographic background, and the gate scores it 0.00--0.07 where it scored 0.16--0.73 on the original; in about six the disc is gone or cannot be told from the background. The gate's hazard cue for this fire class is thus partly a cue for the simulator's rendering style, which agrees with the synthetic-to-real smoke gap reported for wildfire data~\cite{gaba2025wildfiresynthetic}. \textbf{No false stops.} The photographic clean worlds produce \QIRFalse{} false stops in \QIRCleanN{} (QI3 \QIClaimQIThree), so the loss is one of recall, not of precision. \textbf{The generator did not draw fires reliably.} \QIGExcl{} of \QIGGen{} generated fire images (\QIGExclShare) carry no flame colour in the mask, so QI2 is \QIGStopped; the \QIGN{} surviving worlds are a selected subset (\QIGMiss{} missed) and are not evidence about the gate. \textbf{The neutral control was not neutral.} The same masks edited with a neutral prompt push \QICFlips{} of \QICN{} clean twins over the threshold (QI3c \QIClaimQIThreec); by eye the editor paints grey smoke-like plumes into masks shaped like smoke domes, and the three other model families flag almost only the same twins: of the \QIGateFlipsStrict{} twins (all three views scored) that the gate pushes over $\tau$, the other families flag \QIPJudgeInternC{}, \QIPJudgeIdeficsC{} and \QIPJudgeSmolC{} at their own thresholds, and \QIPJudgeInternBoth{}, \QIPJudgeIdeficsBoth{} and \QIPJudgeSmolBoth{} of those flags lie inside the gate's own flips. The flips are therefore mostly correct detections of an editor artifact, not false alarms of the gate on a neutral scene, and they show that mask-guided editing cannot be used as a clean control here. Limits: generated images are not real photographs; the editor and the gate are both Qwen-family models; QI-G reuses XD's fire geometries; one checkpoint, one seed and one prompt per mode; tolerances were not power-derived. The claim that survives is narrow: the registered, pixel-checked protocol shows that a recall loss appears as soon as the simulator's smoke is rendered photographically, which the simulator-only evaluation of the main text could not show.
