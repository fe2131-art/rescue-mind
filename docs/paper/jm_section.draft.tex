\subsection{Study JM: Smoke-Plume Twins}\label{sec:moved-jm}
Post hoc, every residual miss of the left-only and extra-view arms of GM and JK lay in a world with a smoke plume (Fisher $p$ 0.06 and 0.25; Section~\ref{sec:moved-jk}). Smoke volume, more than contrast, drives how well multimodal models find smoke~\cite{qi2026smokebench}. Study JM (registered before any JM world was selected) tests the plume as a cause: \JMTwins{} fresh fire worlds, each built twice, with and without the plume (\texttt{depot.particle\_smoke}; nothing else differs), and the arms \texttt{v36} and \texttt{adapt} (JK's configuration, unchanged) on both twins (failed runs \JMFailed, audit failures \JMAudit).
\emph{Claims.} JM1: the plume raises the misses of v3.6 (deliveries into fire \JMVSmoke{} with the plume, \JMVClear{} without; \JMSmokeVFirst{} pairs unsafe only with the plume against \JMSmokeVSecond{} only without, one-sided exact McNemar $p=\JMSmokeVP$): \JMOneHolds. JM2: in the plume twins the extra views deliver into fewer fires than v3.6 (\JMAdaptSmoke{} against \JMVSmoke; \JMArmSmokeFirst{} against \JMArmSmokeSecond, $p=\JMArmSmokeP$): \JMTwoHolds. JM3: the extra views deliver into at most 4 of the plume twins (\JMAdaptSmoke, upper \JMAdaptSmokeUpper): \JMThreeHolds. Predictions: at most 1 miss of the extra views without the plume (\JMAdaptClear) \JMPredOneHolds; extra views triggered more often with the plume (\JMTrigSmoke{} against \JMTrigClear) \JMPredTwoHolds; median time cost at most 30\,s (\JMTimeCost\,s) \JMPredThreeHolds.
