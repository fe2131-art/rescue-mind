\subsection{Study JK: Suspicion-Triggered Extra Views in the Closed Loop}\label{sec:moved-jk}
Study JI (image level, fresh worlds) found that more views remove more of the gate's misses than a second judge does. The mission already has the mechanism (I-20): after every standard view (front, 0.4\,m left and right) has passed, any score of at least $\tau_{sus}=0.005$ sends the robot to two poses 0.8\,m beside the bay (a third, 0.3\,m closer, is dropped in the registered arm, see below), and an extra view that shows a hazard revokes the approval; no extra view can add an approval. Study JK (registered and stamped before any JK run) runs it as an arm, \texttt{adapt}, at the default thresholds and nothing tuned, on the \JKWorlds{} fire worlds and \JKPlain{} fire-free worlds of study GM (\JKTrials{} runs). The comparators are GM's own runs of the same worlds (v3.6 \JKV{}, left-only \JKLeft{}); the pairing is by world but \emph{not concurrent}, so times are descriptive.
\emph{Claims.} JK1: fewer deliveries into fire than v3.6, one-sided exact McNemar: \JKAdapt{} against \JKV{}, \JKOnlyV{} worlds unsafe only under v3.6 against \JKOnlyAdapt{} only under \texttt{adapt}, $p=\JKP$: \JKOneHolds{} (nearly structural: the extra views can only add refusals). JK2: upper bound at most 10\% (\JKAdaptRate{}, upper \JKAdaptUpper; prediction 4--6 of 100): \JKTwoHolds. JK3: at most 3 false stops of the fire-free worlds, \JKFalseStops{} (v3.6: \JKFalseStopsV): \JKThreeHolds. Predictions: not more deliveries into fire than the left-only arm (\JKLeft{}; \JKOnlyLeft{} worlds unsafe only under left-only, \JKOnlyAdaptL{} only under \texttt{adapt}) \JKPredOneHolds; median time at most 30\,s above v3.6 (\JKTimeAdapt\,s against \JKTimeV\,s) \JKPredTwoHolds; extra views triggered in \JKTriggerShare{} of the \JKReached{} missions that reached them, \JKAllExtras{} of \JKTriggered{} triggered missions took both poses or ended in a hazard refusal at the first (\JKAllExtras{} took both, \JKRefusedEarly{} refused after L2) \JKPredThreeHolds.
Active perception that moves the sensor to resolve occlusion is established (occlusion-aware next-best-view planning for a partly hidden person on a mobile robot~\cite{hu2026oanbv}; conformal, uncertainty-triggered active perception for robot teams~\cite{podolinsky2026coglance}); JK differs in the trigger (the gate's own low score), the fixed two-pose sweep, and a registered, ground-truth-audited closed-loop budget for a hazard gate. Multi-view verification with a VLM to cut false positives~\cite{liang2026zonda}, training-free crops triggered by uncertainty~\cite{wang2026vicropdet} and zooming~\cite{shen2024zoomeye} are related; VLMs are also known not to lower their confidence when occlusion removes the evidence, where a better viewpoint helps more than extra cues~\cite{zhang2026seeingisnt}, and to be misled by content from other views~\cite{park2026multiviewhallu}, which our design avoids by scoring every view alone and combining by a maximum. A warning from test-time scaling applies to our comparison: gains attributed to a perturbation can come from the decoding format, and a matched-budget control is needed~\cite{zheng2026decodingformat}. The extra views of JK add drive time and model calls, and we did not run a matched-budget control in the closed loop (for example the same number of extra looks from the front pose); the image-level evidence that the new poses, not the extra calls, matter is the per-view catch counts of Study JI.
\emph{Pilot findings (registered as Amendments 1--3 before any JK trial).} The mechanics pilot found three defects the unit tests had not: the orchestrator did not pass the flag that forces the extra views; in one fire-free world two runs failed right after the 0.3\,m near pose N (the guard's front-sector rule stopped the robot as it left the pose; wheel odometry drifted 0.91\,m in the other), and N adds no catch on the image-level sets (identical misses and false stops with and without N on XD and JI), so the registered arm omits N; and the independent completion audit required three extra views where the arm schedules two. The audit still fails a run that skips L2 or R2. A fourth clerical correction (Amendment 4) made prediction P3 follow its registered wording.
\emph{Reading.} The extra views are reached and they catch fires in the closed loop. At the default thresholds and without any calibration the arm delivered into fire in \JKAdapt{} of the \JKWorlds{} worlds (upper bound \JKAdaptUpper) against \JKV{} for the submitted look-around, and its \JKPlain{} fire-free missions were all completed (\JKFalseStops{}), so the forecast that the medicine crate's right view would cost availability did not materialise at 0.8\,m on these worlds. Six missions were stopped at the first extra pose, i.e. L2 alone caught them. The pairing with the left-only arm of GL and GM is a draw (\JKOnlyLeft{} against \JKOnlyAdaptL{}), but \texttt{adapt} needs no site calibration and drives only when a standard view scored at least 0.005. JK1 holds nearly by construction (extra views can only add refusals); the informative results are the absolute budget and the absence of false stops. Limits: simulation; the worlds are GM's (one fire geometry, four realism variants); the comparators are GM's runs of the same worlds at another time; the near pose was dropped after the pilot; GM031 and GM055 (and GM013) were still missed.
