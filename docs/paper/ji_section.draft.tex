\subsection{Study JI: Do More Views Beat a Second Judge on Fresh Worlds? Study JC: A Fast Judge That Decides Where to Look}\label{sec:moved-ji}
The factor analysis of study JH (post hoc, one image set) found that more look-around views remove more of the gate's misses than a second judge does. Study JI (registered and stamped before any image was rendered; thresholds frozen from XD, nothing fitted) replays it on \JIFire{} fresh small, partly hidden fires and \JIClean{} fire-free twins, six views each (F, L, R, L2, R2, N), at the image level.
\emph{Claims.} JI1: the gate on six views misses at most 8 of the fires: \JISix{} (\JISixRate, upper \JISixUpper): \JIOneHolds. JI2: six views miss fewer fires than the standard three (\JIThree{}; \JIOnlyThree{} fires missed only with three views, \JIOnlySix{} only with six, one-sided exact McNemar $p=\JIP$): \JITwoHolds. JI3: at most 5 false stops with six views, \JISixFS{} (three views: \JIThreeFS): \JIThreeHolds. JI4: gate plus the InternVL3 veto on six views misses at most 3 with at most 6 false stops, \JIVetoMiss{} and \JIVetoFS: \JIFourHolds. With all four judges as vetoes: \JIAllMiss{} missed, \JIAllFS{} false stops (descriptive).
\emph{Reading.} (Written after the numbers: whether the view count replicates on fresh worlds, what it does to the earlier reading that a second judge adds little, and the limits: image level, simulator, one generator.)

\textbf{Study JC (the judge's job is speed).} Study JH used the judge as a redundant veto. JC gives it a role the gate does not have: a sentinel on the three standard views that decides whether the three extra views are worth driving; it can neither approve nor refuse, so a sentinel false alarm costs time, not a delivery (Proposition~2 and the gate's false-stop budget are unchanged). The sentinel is the judge with the lowest median scoring latency in JI (\JCPrimary{}, \JCLatSent\,s per image; InternVL3: \JCLatIntern\,s). On the same worlds, the gate misses \JCThreeMiss{} with three views and \JCSixMiss{} with six; with the sentinel as trigger it misses \JCSentMiss{} at a mean of \JCSentViews{} views per clean mission (three views: 3, six: 6; extra views triggered on \JCTrigFire{} of the fires and \JCTrigClean{} of the clean worlds), \JCSentFS{} false stops; the gate's own low-score trigger gives \JCOwnMiss{} at \JCOwnViews{} views. Claims: JC1 (misses within 3 of always-six) \JCOneHolds; JC2 (at most 4.0 views on clean worlds) \JCTwoHolds; JC3 (at most 5 false stops) \JCThreeHolds. Predictions: fewer misses than always three \JCPredOneHolds; faster than always six in the estimated extra time (\JCSentSecs\,s against \JCSixSecs\,s per clean mission, drive time borrowed from GL, not measured) \JCPredTwoHolds.
\emph{Reading.} (Written after the numbers: say whether speed bought views at constant recall, whether the judge was fast, and that a closed-loop version is untested; GPU memory beside the gate fits only the small judge.)
