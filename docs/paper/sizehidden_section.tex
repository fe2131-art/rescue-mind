\subsection{Is the Hidden-Fire Deficit Only a Size Effect? (post hoc)}\label{sec:moved-sizehidden}
Multimodal models lose accuracy on small objects independently of other factors~\cite{zhang2024perceptuallimit}, recognise tiny patterns only above about a dozen pixels~\cite{li2026lastpixel}, lag far behind humans on occlusion~\cite{liu2025obench} and on spatial reasoning across robot camera views~\cite{feng2025mvrobobench}; SmokeBench~\cite{qi2026smokebench} reports that smoke volume matters more than contrast for multimodal models, so a reviewer can ask whether the gate's misses on \emph{hidden} fires are only misses on \emph{small} fires. We checked on the two registered image sets (\texttt{scripts/size\_vs\_hidden.py}; JI with \SHJIN{} fire images and XD with \SHXDN{}, thresholds as registered, no new data). The label \texttt{fire\_pixels} is the area of the fire-only render, i.e. the size the fire would have with no occluder; \texttt{hazard\_visibility} says whether the fire is visible in the actual frame. The result is that the two are confounded by construction: every hidden or ambiguous fire falls in the smallest area bin (up to \SHJIMax{} pixels in JI, \SHXDMax{} in XD; the perceivable-fire rule of the paper starts at 614), and there the flag rate is \SHJIVis{} for visible fires (\SHJIVisN{} images) against \SHJIHid{} for hidden ones (\SHJIHidN{}) in JI, and \SHXDVis{} (\SHXDVisN{}) against \SHXDHid{} (\SHXDHidN{}) in XD. Stratified by 12 equal-count area bins, the Mantel--Haenszel odds ratio of visible against hidden is \SHJIOR{} (95\% interval \SHJIORLow--\SHJIORHigh) in JI and \SHXDOR{} (\SHXDORLow--\SHXDORHigh) in XD: at the same potential size we cannot show that hiding lowers the flag rate, and we do not claim it. The reading is therefore the plain one: the hard class is a small-fire class, and the look-around works because a side view enlarges or uncovers the fire (the 0.8\,m side views catch most of the fires that the front view misses, Study JI). A matched-area study that composes one real fire at several sizes behind and beside an occluder would separate the two factors; it is not done.
