\subsection{Study JK: Suspicion-Triggered Extra Views in the Closed Loop}\label{sec:moved-jk}
Study JI (image level, fresh worlds) found that more views remove more of the gate's misses than a second judge does. The mission already has the mechanism (I-20): after every standard view (front, 0.4\,m left and right) has passed, any score of at least $\tau_{sus}=0.005$ sends the robot to two poses 0.8\,m beside the bay and one 0.3\,m closer, and an extra view that shows a hazard revokes the approval; no extra view can add an approval. Study JK (registered and stamped before any JK run) runs it as an arm, \texttt{adapt}, at the default thresholds and nothing tuned, on the \JKWorlds{} fire worlds and \JKPlain{} fire-free worlds of study GM (\JKTrials{} runs). The comparators are GM's own runs of the same worlds (v3.6 \JKV{}, left-only \JKLeft{}); the pairing is by world but \emph{not concurrent}, so times are descriptive.
\emph{Claims.} JK1: fewer deliveries into fire than v3.6, one-sided exact McNemar: \JKAdapt{} against \JKV{}, \JKOnlyV{} worlds unsafe only under v3.6 against \JKOnlyAdapt{} only under \texttt{adapt}, $p=\JKP$: \JKOneHolds{} (nearly structural: the extra views can only add refusals). JK2: upper bound at most 10\% (\JKAdaptRate{}, upper \JKAdaptUpper; prediction 4--6 of 100): \JKTwoHolds. JK3: at most 3 false stops of the fire-free worlds, \JKFalseStops{} (v3.6: \JKFalseStopsV): \JKThreeHolds. Predictions: not more deliveries into fire than the left-only arm (\JKLeft{}; \JKOnlyLeft{} worlds unsafe only under left-only, \JKOnlyAdaptL{} only under \texttt{adapt}) \JKPredOneHolds; median time at most 30\,s above v3.6 (\JKTimeAdapt\,s against \JKTimeV\,s) \JKPredTwoHolds; extra views triggered in \JKTriggerShare{} of the \JKReached{} missions that reached them, \JKAllExtras{} of \JKTriggered{} triggered missions took all three poses \JKPredThreeHolds.
