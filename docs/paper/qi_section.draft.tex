\subsection{Study QI: Generated Images Scored by the Frozen Gate}\label{sec:moved-qi}
The paper's weakest points are the simulator-to-reality gap and the small hard-class sample. Study QI (registered before any generated study image or score; Amendments 1--4 recorded before any score) uses an image-editing model, Qwen-Image-2.1-Turbo (7\,B, 8 steps, fp16, one checkpoint, one seed per image), to make the depot test images of XD look photographic and to draw fires the simulator did not render, and scores them with the \emph{frozen} gate (Qwen3.5-9B, v3.6, $\tau=0.088$ from XD's own calibration; nothing is tuned). Related work uses generative editing to enlarge robot training data~\cite{yu2023rosie}, to translate simulator frames toward real ones~\cite{alhaija2025cosmostransfer,samak2025sim2realdiffusion} and to make simulated scenes more realistic for policy evaluation~\cite{benarous2025spie,li2024simpler}; synthetic smoke datasets show a clear synthetic-to-real gap~\cite{gaba2025wildfiresynthetic}. We know of no earlier use of a generative editor to stress-test a VLM safety gate under a registered, pixel-checked protocol; the claim is that narrow.
\emph{Sets.} QI-R: all XD test frames (views F, L, R) edited by one materials prompt (\QIRN{} perceivable hard-class fire worlds, \QIRCleanN{} clean worlds). QI-G: the clean twin of each XD fire world with a fire drawn only inside the fire world's own dilated fire mask (pixels outside the mask unchanged, checked; an image with less than 2\% flame colour in the mask is excluded and listed). QI-C: the same twins and masks with a neutral prompt (the editor's artifacts alone). A world is scored by the maximum over its three views; a view that was not drawn keeps the clean twin's own score (Amendment 3). Descriptive extensions (Amendment 2): a larger fire (G2) and two orange non-fire look-alikes (K1 vest, K2 warning lamp).
\emph{Claims.} QI1: the miss on the realised hard fires is at most 20 of 126 (XD's own: \QIBaseMiss{} of \QIBaseN): \QIRMiss{} of \QIRN{} (exact upper bound \QIRUpper), \QIClaimQI1. QI2: the generated fire is missed in at most 20\% of worlds: \QIGMiss{} of \QIGN{} (\QIGRate, upper \QIGUpper), \QIClaimQI2. QI3: at most 5 false stops among the realised clean worlds, \QIRFalse{} of \QIRCleanN{}, \QIClaimQI3; control edits cross $\tau$ in \QICFlips{} of \QICN{} twins (at most 5\%), \QIClaimQI3c.
