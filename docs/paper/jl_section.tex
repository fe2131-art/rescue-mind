\subsection{Study JL: The Closed-Loop Extra Views on Fresh Worlds, With a Concurrent Control}\label{sec:moved-jl}
Study JK measured the suspicion-triggered extra views on the worlds of GM against GM's earlier runs of the same worlds. Study JL (registered and stamped before any JL world was selected) removes both limits: its \JLWorlds{} fire worlds and \JLPlain{} fire-free worlds are fresh (GM's generator and realism variants, the seed \texttt{JL-2026}, no earlier world reused), and the arms \texttt{v36} and \texttt{adapt} (JK's final configuration: L2 and R2 at 0.8\,m, no near pose, nothing tuned) were run interleaved in one session (\JLTrials{} runs).
\emph{Claims.} JL1: fewer deliveries into fire than v3.6, one-sided exact McNemar: \JLAdapt{} against \JLV{}, \JLOnlyV{} worlds unsafe only under v3.6 against \JLOnlyAdapt{} only under \texttt{adapt}, $p=\JLP$: \JLOneHolds. JL2: upper bound at most 10\% (\JLAdaptRate{}, upper \JLAdaptUpper; prediction 2--5 of 100): \JLTwoHolds. JL3: at most 3 false stops of the fire-free worlds, \JLFalseStops{} (v3.6: \JLFalseStopsV): \JLThreeHolds. Predictions: median time at most 30\,s above v3.6 (\JLTimeAdapt\,s against \JLTimeV\,s, concurrent) \JLPredOneHolds; extra views triggered in \JLTriggerShare{} of the \JLReached{} missions that reached them, \JLAllExtras{} of \JLTriggered{} triggered missions took both poses and \JLRefusedEarly{} refused after the first \JLPredTwoHolds; false stops not more than v3.6's plus 2 \JLPredThreeHolds.
\emph{Reading.} JK replicated. On fresh worlds and against v3.6 runs interleaved in the same session, the extra views delivered into fire in \JLAdapt{} worlds against \JLV{} (\JLOnlyV{} worlds unsafe only under v3.6, \JLOnlyAdapt{} only under the extra views), almost the same as JK's \JKAdapt{} against \JKV{}, so the reduction is not an artefact of reusing GM's worlds or of a historical control. The budget holds with room (upper \JLAdaptUpper{} against the registered 10\%), no fire-free world was refused (\JLFalseStops), and the median time cost is \JLTimeAdapt\,s against \JLTimeV\,s. The extra views were triggered in \JLTriggerShare{} of the missions that reached them, and the remaining misses are worlds where neither the default views nor the 0.8\,m views show the fire. Limits: simulation, one depot generator and one fire geometry (a small fire behind the requested supply), and the configuration was fixed after JK's pilot; JL1 is close to guaranteed by construction, since extra views can only add refusals, so JL2 and JL3 carry the information.
