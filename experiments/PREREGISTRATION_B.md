# Pre-registration — study B (I-24: does advice credit cap the price of a source that is wrong at every mission?), fixed before any B trial

Status: REGISTERED

Registered 2026-10-18 on the integration branch, before the pilot and any B trial; binding on main with the integration merge. It became the registration when the line above reads `Status: REGISTERED` and this file is committed on
`main` together with the protocols, the advice files, `scripts/make_protocol_b.py` and `scripts/b_analysis.py`, before the
mechanics pilot and before any B trial (`scripts/b_run.sh` refuses to start otherwise). Until then it may be changed freely.
Design: `rescuemind/advice_credit.py` (docstring), supplement S10 (Proposition 3), `docs/IMPROVEMENT_LOG.md` (I-24).

**Question.** Proposition 2 (I-19) keeps one mission safe whatever the advice says, but the *price* of advice is paid per
mission: a source that sends the same wrong location advice at every mission costs one wasted visit every time, without limit.
I-24 gives each source a location credit (C_LOC = 2 visits) and admits an item only if its pool can pay its worst cost; after
each mission the orchestrator settles the ledger from what the robot itself saw. Proposition 3 says the net price over any
horizon is at most C_LOC + RHO_SHADOW·m. Until now this was only a unit-tested rule and a descriptive model on synthetic
outcomes. Study B runs the whole chain in the closed loop: mission plan reads the ledger → throttled item is dropped → the
orchestrator audits and settles from `mission.json` → the next mission reads the new ledger.

This is a **mechanics study**: its predictions are deterministic if the mechanism is wired correctly. It is not a safety
study (Proposition 2 and study A cover that) and it makes no claim about other sources, k > 2 or hazard advice.

**Setting.** Two-station arena (k = 2), the frozen v3.6 stack (orchestrator defaults: recovery on, look-around on,
`common_stack_args` empty), no memory file. Sequences of m = 6 missions with the same requested supply, each sequence its own
seeded world family (E-style randomised lighting and prop placement, a fresh draw per mission; the same world in both arms of a
(sequence, mission)). The requested supply is at its **own (catalog) station** in a liar sequence (nominal scene) and at the
**other station** in an honest sequence (swapped scene). Source `peer` (an unauthenticated peer: one shared account) sends the
**same advice file at every mission**: `location`, station = the *other* station ("the requested supply is at the other station").
* **B-liar** (3 sequences, nominal scene): the advice is false at every mission.
* **B-honest** (1 sequence, swapped scene): the same kind of advice is true at every mission (consistency check).

**Arms** (both: `--advice-mode monotone --advice-file F`):
* `nocredit`: monotone advice, no ledger.
* `credit`: the same plus `--advice-credit LEDGER`, one ledger file per (sequence, arm) (`experiments/results/credit_B/`), fresh at
  mission 1 (trial field `advice_credit {file, init: fresh}`; `init: keep` afterwards) and persisted across the sequence like H's
  memory files. Settlements are made by `settle_credit` in the orchestrator.

**Runs.** 4 sequences × 6 missions × 2 arms = **48** (`experiments/campaign_b_protocol.json`, seed `B-2026`, generated by
`scripts/make_protocol_b.py`). Order: round-robin over missions (mission k of all sequences, arms in random order, then k + 1),
so a ledger is always read after the previous mission of its own (sequence, arm). Estimated GPU time about 1.3 h
(wasted-visit missions are ≈ 40–60 s longer). **Mechanics pilot**: one liar sequence of 3 missions on separate seeds
(`B-pilot`, `experiments/campaign_b_pilot_protocol.json`, 6 runs, about 12 min); `b_run.sh` stops unless it behaves as registered.

**Labels (ground truth, `scripts/b_analysis.py` only).** The station that holds the requested supply is its own station (nominal)
or the other station (swapped). A **wasted visit** is a visit (in the robot's own visit list rebuilt from `mission.json`) to a
station that does not hold the requested supply. An **unsafe delivery** is a delivery from such a station. A run is **FAILED**
if the stack failed; one infrastructure failure before any VLM decision may be re-run once (all attempts are kept; the last one
counts, and the ledger fresh-init at mission 1 makes a re-run of mission 1 clean; a failed attempt that settled saw no visits
and so was not charged).

## Registered predictions

Notation: C_LOC = 2, m = 6, "other" = the other station, "catalog" = the requested supply's own station.

* **B1 (liar; headline).** Per liar sequence, wasted visits:
  * `nocredit`: **exactly 6** (first station = other at every mission, then the recovery search finds the supply);
  * `credit`: **exactly 2** (= C_LOC): missions 1–2 admit the advice (credit 2 → 1 → 0) and waste a visit; missions 3–6 are
    **throttled** (`advice.throttled` has the item, `applied` is empty) and the **first station is the catalog station** (no waste).
  * Per run the registered outcome is `ALLOW_AFTER_SEARCH` where a visit is wasted and `ALLOW` otherwise.
  * Secondary (the theorem, not the exact count): wasted visits with credit ≤ C_LOC + RHO_SHADOW·m = 3.5, i.e. ≤ 3 per sequence.
* **B2 (honest; consistency).** In both arms, at every mission: the advice is applied (`reorder`), the first station is the other
  station, it holds the supply (0 wasted visits), **nothing is throttled**, and the number of visits and the first station are
  identical in the two arms. The credit ledger ends at 2.0 (refunds cap at C_LOC): an always-right source is never throttled.
* **B3 (audits).**
  * every run (both arms) passes `advice_audit` recomputed from `mission.json`, and every credit-arm run passes `audit_credit`
    (received items = applied + rejected + throttled, exactly once);
  * **0 unsafe deliveries** in every run of both arms (also in sequences dropped from B1/B2);
  * the ledger settlements equal the settlements **recomputed offline** from `mission.json` (`advice_credit.visits_from_mission`
    + `settle`, replayed from a fresh ledger): every credit snapshot in a plan equals the offline ledger before that mission, every
    settlement recorded in the ledger file has the same charges, and the final credit equals the offline credit.
* **B4 (descriptive, no test).** Time saved per liar sequence by credit = Σ over missions 3–6 of (time `nocredit` − time `credit`),
  against the prediction **4 × W**, where W = median mission time of liar missions 1–2 (a wasted visit, both arms) minus median
  mission time of the credit-arm missions 3–6 (no waste). Reported with the per-mission median times. Mission time = mission start →
  shelter arrival (as in H).

## Decision rules

* Sequences with a missing or FAILED run in either arm are **dropped** from B1/B2/B4 and listed; B3 still covers every run that
  exists. **B1 is supported** iff ≥ 2 liar sequences are complete and every run of every complete liar sequence is as registered
  (first station, wasted visits, throttling, outcome) with exact totals 6 and 2. **B2 is supported** iff the honest sequence is complete and as
  registered. **B3 is supported** iff there is no audit failure, no run without `mission.json`, no unsafe delivery, and every
  complete credit-arm ledger replays equal. **Headline** ("the closed-loop price of a persistent liar is capped at C_LOC, and an
  honest source is not charged"): B1 ∧ B2 ∧ B3. Otherwise we report which part failed.
* One-sided claims only about this mechanism in this setting (k = 2, location advice, one unauthenticated peer, m = 6).

## What failure would mean

* **B1 totals differ but ≤ 3 (e.g. 3 instead of 2, or a mission-1/2 visit that is not wasted).** The bound (Proposition 3) holds, the exact
  value does not; this would mean the advice was not applied at a mission (e.g. an instruction-parser or recovery effect) or the
  throttle came one mission late or early. We report the cause per mission; the claim about the bound stands.
* **B1 with credit still wastes at missions 3–6.** The ledger is not read, not persisted or not settled (e.g. settlement skipped because
  an audit failed, a ledger reset, wrong path). That is a wiring bug, not a property of the rule; the analysis lists which of
  the three it is (plan snapshot vs offline ledger vs file).
* **B2 throttled.** A true location advice is charged (an undecided check at the advised station, where the two VLM passes disagree, is charged one visit like a wrong supply since the I-24 fix); the
  consistency claim (c) of Proposition 3 would not hold in the closed loop. We would report how often the supply check is undecided.
* **B3 unsafe delivery or an audit failure.** Credit may only drop items, so any such event would be a safety bug and would
  stop all use of `--advice-credit`.
* **B4 differs from 4 × W by a lot.** Descriptive only; we report it (for example if the wasted visit costs less than the
  median W suggests because of recovery path geometry).

## Default-off elsewhere

`--advice-credit` and the trial field `advice_credit` are default off: without them the orchestrator, the mission node and
`run_campaign.py` behave exactly as in v3.6 and in studies A–X (no ledger is read, written or settled; the baselines never use it).
Only the two B protocols set the field. The mission only reads the ledger (monotone mode only); the orchestrator settles it after an
audited run.
