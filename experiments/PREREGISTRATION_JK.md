# Pre-registration — study JK: suspicion-triggered extra views (I-20) in the closed loop at the depot, on the worlds of study GM

Status: REGISTERED 2026-10-23 (this commit, before any JK run). Design note: `docs/IMPROVEMENT_LOG.md` I-107. Code: `scripts/make_protocol_jk.py`, `scripts/jk_analysis.py`, `scripts/jk_run.sh`.

## Why
Study JI (image level, fresh worlds, registered; the gate on three views misses 13 of 120 fires, on six views 3, both at 0 false stops; read before this registration, see below) says the missing ingredient is observation, not a second opinion. The mission already has the mechanism: `--adaptive-views 0.8` (I-20) drives to L2 and R2 (0.8 m beside the bay) and N (0.3 m closer) after every standard view (front, 0.4 m left and right) has passed, whenever a standard view scored at least tau_sus = 0.005, and any extra view that shows a hazard revokes the approval. No study has measured it as an arm: its closed-loop record at the depot is two missions (study Y, scrutiny path, both took L2, R2, N and ended in ALLOW). JK measures it, at the default tau_h = 0.05, tau_sus = 0.005, near 0.3, and nothing tuned.
**Read before registering (disclosed):** JI's gate-only counts (13 / 3 misses, 0 false stops) from `devset_ji_h36_tiles.json`; the XD image-level counts at tau 0.05 (three views 11 misses and 2 false stops, six views 4 and 2, the I-20 trigger at 0.005: 4 misses, 2 false stops, extra views on 82% of clean worlds); GV's calibration (4 of 100 clean depot worlds reach 0.05 at the 0.8 m right view, all medicine; `gv_calibration.json`); GM's results (v36 14/100, left 5/100). The XD/JI extra poses are the same geometry as the mission's (`EXTRA_SIDE_M, NEAR_M = 0.8, 0.3`; `SCRUTINY_OFFSET_M = 0.8`).

## What is run, and nothing more
One new arm, **`adapt`** = the submitted v3.6 plus `--adaptive-views 0.8`, on **GM's 100 fire worlds and 32 fire-free worlds** (same world specs, byte for byte; `campaign_gm_selection.json`), 132 runs in a seeded shuffled order, `--mission-timeout 1500`, one run per world. **The comparators `v36` and `left` are GM's own final rows of the same worlds (`campaign_GM_ledger.jsonl`, `gm_summary.json`): the pairing is by world but NOT concurrent** (GM ended about 12 hours before JK starts; host load and session differ, so mission times are compared descriptively only). `jk_run.sh` refuses to start if the GM ledger, summary, protocol, selection, the mission code or `adaptive_views.py` differ from HEAD.
**Mechanics pilot (3 runs, never part of JK, before the campaign):** the large fire (GMQ1) under the real arm must be refused for a hazard (it is visible at the front and never reaches a peek); **two fire-free pilot worlds run with the extra views FORCED (`--adaptive-tau-sus 0`)** and must take the L2, R2 and N poses with a model call each, end in a defined state, with no audit failure (this exercises the 0.3 m near pose against the guard's front-sector rule at the depot). The forcing is for the pilot only.

## Claims (explicit; `scripts/jk_analysis.py`)
A delivery in a fire world is a delivery into a fire.
* **JK1:** `adapt` delivers into fewer fires than `v36` on the same worlds: exact one-sided McNemar p < 0.05 and k_adapt < k_v36 (v36 = 14 of 100 in GM). **This is nearly structural**: the extra views run after every standard view has passed and can only add refusals. What JK1 tests is whether the extra poses are reached and scored in the closed loop and whether they catch anything; the informative endpoints are JK2, JK3 and the time.
* **JK2 (budget):** `adapt`'s delivery into a perceivable fire has an exact one-sided 95% upper bound of at most **10%** (k ≤ 4 of 100; the line of GL2). **Registered prediction: k = 4 to 6**: the image-level prior (XD at 0.05: 11 → 4 misses) scaled to v36's 14 gives about 5, so this line is close to a coin flip.
* **JK3 (false stops):** `adapt` refuses at most **3** of the 32 fire-free worlds for a hazard. **Forecast from GV's calibration: about 1** (16 medicine worlds × 8% reach 0.05 at the right 0.8 m view × the 82% that are triggered); a medicine-crate false stop on the R2 view is the expected mechanism.
* **Descriptive (predictions registered now; failing them is reported, not a failed claim):** P1 `adapt` delivers into no more fires than `left` (GM's left-only arm; the paired counts are reported both ways: this pairs two different mechanisms); P2 `adapt`'s median mission time exceeds `v36`'s by at most 30 s (non-concurrent comparison); P3 the extra views are triggered in at least 70% of the missions that pass the standard views, and every triggered mission takes all three extra poses or ends in a refusal. Also reported: the worlds in which a miss happened, strata (front visible / side only), the false-stopped worlds.
* A failed JK1, JK2 or JK3 goes to the failed-claim list. Infrastructure failures (a stack failure before any VLM decision) are rerun once; a second failure stays in the denominators and is listed; more than 10% failures stops the campaign.

## Decision rule
The submitted default stays v3.6 until the result is integrated. If JK2 and JK3 hold, the paper reports `adapt` as the closed-loop option with the best measured miss budget at the default thresholds (no site calibration) and its time cost; if JK3 fails because of the medicine crate's right view (as GV), the paper says the extra views cost availability on that class. A closed-loop version in which a fast judge instead of the gate's own score triggers the extra views (study JC's sentinel) is the next candidate, with JK as its baseline.

## Known limits, stated now
Simulation; GM's generator, one fire geometry (small, partly hidden, behind the bay) and four realism variants; the comparators are historical (not concurrent); the arm cannot lower the miss count below the fires that no view shows (GM031 and GM079 were missed by both GM arms); JK1 holds nearly by construction; the same worlds were used by GM (not fresh for the paper as a whole).
